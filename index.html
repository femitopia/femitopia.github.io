Femitopia - A place to belong, explore, and create

A lesbian-centered queer sanctuary on the coast of Oaxaca, surrounded by nature, beautiful beaches, and a community of friendly queer women.

Come for a holiday, stay for a few weeks, or spend a few months with us. Explore beaches, waterfalls, and hot springs. Join workshops, volunteer on the land, learn new skills, or simply relax and enjoy being surrounded by people who understand you.

Femitopia is a place to connect with nature, with others, and with yourself, and to be part of a growing community where you can feel at home.

A Lesbian-Centered Queer Space

Femitopia is a space centered around lesbians and the wider lesbian community. We welcome queer women and those who feel part of this community, including trans and nonbinary people who identify with or feel at home in the lesbian community.

It's a place to find connection, friendship, freedom, and a sense of belonging, without having to explain who you are or feel like the odd one out.

Come on your own, with a partner, or with friends. Stay for a few days or a few months. You don't need to be outgoing, attend every activity, or contribute to the project. You can simply be here.

Discover Zipolite

A hidden gem on Mexico's Pacific Coast

Zipolite, located in Oaxaca, Mexico, is known for its peaceful atmosphere and nudist-friendly beach. Its 1.75 km of sandy coastline is perfect for swimming and surfing. Enjoy the consistent tropical weather. Visit charming towns like Mazunte, or head to Huatulco Bay and Puerto Escondido for more adventures. Spot wildlife such as dolphins, and migrating whales.

Living in Harmony with Nature

Femitopia is built on sustainable principles - we care for the land as much as we care for our community.

💧

Water Recycling

We use a Biotar system to recycle gray and black water, which is then used to nourish our banana, papaya, and maracuyá plants.

🌳

Edible Forest

We have started an edible forest with mango, avocado, lemon, and coconut trees, growing food while restoring the land.

☀️

Solar Energy

Our solar panels supply the electricity we need, reducing our carbon footprint and keeping us off the grid.

🌧️

Rainwater Collection

Our roof is specially designed to collect rainwater, providing a natural and sustainable water source for the community.

Plan Your Visit

Practical information for your trip to Femitopia

Location

Femitopia is located near Zipolite on the Oaxaca coast of Mexico. The area is known for its beautiful beaches, tropical climate, and welcoming atmosphere.

Getting Here

The nearest major airports are in Puerto Escondido and Huatulco. From there, you can take ground transportation to Zipolite. Contact us for specific directions and transport recommendations.

What to Bring

Light, breathable clothing for tropical weather, sunscreen, insect repellent, comfortable walking shoes, and any personal items you need. We're off-grid, so plan accordingly.

Environment

Expect warm tropical weather, insects, and natural surroundings. We're off-grid with solar electricity and rainwater collection. Swimming conditions vary by beach, so ask us about current conditions.
